WELCOME TO THE CHRIS COUNCIL

HOW THE COUNCIL WORKS

The Chris Council is a community-driven platform where members use Council Points (CP) to propose challenges, vote on the challenges they want to see fulfilled, and follow completed challenges in a permanent public history. This page explains the system before you enter the interactive Council.

What is The Chris Council?

The Council gives the community a structured way to suggest things Chris should do. Instead of challenges being selected randomly, members participate through Council Points, challenge submissions, and community voting. The active challenge list shows what the community is currently considering, while completed challenges remain part of the Council's history.

The basic idea: earn or receive CP → submit a challenge → the community votes → the highest eligible challenge rises to the top → Chris fulfills it → the challenge is marked completed.

How Council Points (CP) Work

CP are the Council's internal participation points. They are used to take actions within the platform rather than representing cash or a promise of monetary value.

  • New-member bonus: eligible new members receive the one-time 60 CP login bonus.
  • Challenge creation: submitting a challenge costs 20 CP.
  • Rewarded ads: the site currently displays “Rewarded Ads Coming Soon”; CP should only be credited when the future ad system verifies a valid reward.
  • Administration: authorized administrators can make controlled CP adjustments through the admin system.

How Challenges Are Created

A signed-in member opens Create Challenge, enters a clear challenge title and description, and submits it for 20 CP. The creation request is handled through the Council's Supabase database function rather than simply changing a number in the browser.

Good challenge submissions

  • Clearly explain what the challenge is.
  • Give enough detail for the community to understand the idea.
  • Keep the proposal appropriate for the Council community.
  • Avoid duplicate or previously completed challenges.

How Community Voting Works

Active challenges are displayed in the Challenge Hub. Signed-in members can vote for the active proposals they support. The dashboard's Top 10 Challenges list is based on active challenges with the most votes.

Voting is handled through the Council's database system so the vote action is not supposed to depend on a fake browser-only counter.

What Happens When a Challenge Is Completed?

When an authorized administrator marks a challenge as completed, it leaves the active challenge system and appears in the completed history. Its completion information can be retained with the challenge record.

This keeps the active list focused on proposals that can still receive community attention and gives the Council a permanent record of what has already happened.

Product Ideas

The Council can also collect community ideas for products or creations. Members can propose an idea, and the community can use likes to show which ideas are most interesting.

Product Ideas are separate from challenges: a product idea is a community suggestion for something to create, while a challenge is a proposal for something Chris should do.

Community Chat

Community Chat is the place for members to discuss Council activity and ideas. Keep conversations constructive and respectful, and do not use the chat to bypass Council rules or moderation.

Rules & Community Standards

  • Use the Council honestly. Do not attempt to manipulate votes, CP balances, or database records.
  • Only submit challenges and ideas that are appropriate for the community.
  • Do not spam duplicate submissions.
  • Respect other members when voting, chatting, or discussing ideas.
  • Administrative controls are reserved for authorized administrators.
  • Completed challenges remain historical records and are not intended to be returned to active voting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to vote?

Yes. Voting and challenge creation require you to be signed in.

How much does it cost to create a challenge?

Creating a challenge costs 20 CP.

Can I create a challenge without CP?

No. The Council requires the 20 CP creation cost before a challenge can be submitted.

What does the Top 10 list show?

It shows active challenges ranked by their current vote count.

Can a completed challenge come back?

No. Completed challenges are intended to remain in the permanent history rather than returning to active voting.

Are rewarded ads available right now?

Not yet. The current site says “Rewarded Ads Coming Soon” until the ad system is properly approved and connected.
